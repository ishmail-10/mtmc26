# MTMC 26 — Student Community & Discussion Forum

An unofficial, privacy-first student forum designed exclusively for **MTMC MBBS Batch 2026**.

---

## 🏛️ Boards & Sections

1. **🤫 Anonymous Wall**:
   * Ask doubts, share stress, or post queries 100% anonymously without fear of judgment.
   * Assigns randomized pseudonyms per thread (e.g. `Anonymous #42`) to keep your real identity hidden from peers.

2. **📚 Studies & Viva Prep**:
   * Anatomy (dissection hall tips, relations, mnemonics), Physiology (practical experiments, calculations), and Biochemistry.
   * High-yield viva questions bank and peer answers.

3. **🛍️ Batch Marketplace (Buy & Sell)**:
   * Peer-to-peer marketplace to pass down BD Chaurasia textbooks, Guyton, bone sets (osteology), dissection kits, and aprons within the batch.

4. **🔍 Lost & Found**:
   * Report or claim misplaced stethoscopes, student IDs, keys, or white aprons in Lecture Theatres, Dissection Halls, or Central Library.

5. **📢 CR & Batch Notices**:
   * Official updates and schedule adjustments from Class Representatives.

---

## 🎨 Features
- **Light & Dark Mode**: One-tap toggle between sleek dark obsidian and crisp white aesthetic.
- **Upvotes & Threaded Comments**: Real-time interaction with comments and likes.
- **Search & Sort**: Filter by keywords, "Latest", or "Hot 🔥" discussions.
- **Verification Gateway**: Pre-structured login modal for upcoming roll number authentication.
- **PWA Offline Support**: Works offline on iOS, Android, and Desktop.
